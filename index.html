Alliance operation

The admin creates a group and shares an invite link. People who open it join as members; the admin can make them strategists (see everyone's troops, TS and TC) or admins.

Confederacies

One per line, e.g. “War All: War, WarZ, WarL”. “War All” stays on top; other confeds are sorted by population.

Troop speeds

Every tribe's unit speeds are built in and multiplied by the server speed (0 = automatic from the server address). TC = hero speed (14 at x1).
