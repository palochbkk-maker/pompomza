Artifact information & closest villages

Top 3 closest villages per artifact; best one highlighted per troop profile.

Villages come from the daily map.sql snapshot. Tournament square (TS) and Treasury levels are not in map.sql: type them into the table (saved in this browser; use “Copy village info” to share them via config/village-info.json). Arrival = now + travel time; speeds are simplified estimates.

Plan by AI

The plan is made automatically from the saved troops. It picks a sweeper (ghost first), a catapult village and a TC village with a hero for each target, never using an army twice. Press ✕ on a village that is not ready and the next closest takes its place.

Villages & troops parser

Paste the game's “Village overview → Troops” table. Type the TC and TS level for each village that has them (0 = none). Villages are linked to the map by name; type x|y if not found.

Add a village manually

No paste needed: choose the tribe, fill in the village row and press Add. This is separate from the paste parser above.

Hero speed

Set your hero's speed manually, or pick your horse and equipment. It is used for the TC travel times of your villages.

Edit a member's data

Admins can open any group member's troops, TC, TS and hero settings. Changes are saved to that member.

Alliance operation

The admin creates a group and shares an invite link. People who open it join as members; the admin can make them strategists (see everyone's troops, TS and TC) or admins.

Alliance troops

Offensive troops, TC and TS of everyone in the group, whether parsed or added manually. For strategists and admins.

Confederacies

Create groups of allied alliances. Open the list under a confederacy and tick its alliances. “War All” stays on top; other confederacies are sorted by population.
